TradeBot AI Evolution Timeline
==============================

Added a new AI Intelligence -> EVOLUTION tab.

It uses the existing /v10/operator/status response and displays:
- Current autonomous generation and operating mode
- Promotion and rollback counts
- Current candidate and stability progress
- Exact before/after parameter changes
- Evidence-backed learning reasons
- Account-equity checkpoints captured at operator actions
- Full promotion/rollback audit timeline

No backend or live-trading behaviour was changed.
Historical sections will populate after the first autonomous promotion or rollback is recorded.
