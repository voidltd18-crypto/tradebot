V16.1 AI Portfolio UI

Frontend-only deployment.

Adds an AI PORTFOLIO tab inside AI Intelligence and calls:
  GET /v16/portfolio/status

Displays:
- V16 plan status
- qualified and scanned candidates
- capital deployed and cash reserve
- ranked allocations
- confidence, quality and portfolio score
- capacity, slots and buying power
- why cash is retained

The existing CEO, Board, Brain, Market, Research, Symbols, Rules,
Evolution, Memory, Scientist and Operations pages remain intact.
